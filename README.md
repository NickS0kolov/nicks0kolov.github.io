# Nick Sokolov — AI Research

Learning how machines learn.

[Visit the website](https://nicks0kolov.github.io/)

I'm Nick Sokolov, an ML research engineer working on multimodal learning and embodied AI. I focus on vision-language-action models and robotic manipulation, and investigate how AI models perceive, reason, and represent the world.

This site brings together my research notes, model experiments, and technical articles. Explore explanations of model architectures, implementations, visualizations, and experimental results, with companion notebooks on Kaggle.

## Read and explore

- [From Pixels to Words](https://nicks0kolov.github.io/projects/image-captioning/) — how image-captioning models evolved from CNN–LSTM pipelines to attention and Transformer decoders.
- [Vision Transformer, from scratch](https://nicks0kolov.github.io/projects/vision-transformer/) — build a ViT step by step, train it on CIFAR-10, and inspect its attention maps.
